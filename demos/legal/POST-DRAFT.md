# Unpublished post draft

Legal intake shouldn't begin with a hunt through attachments.

We built The matter room: a Selerim concept that organizes intake documents,
extracts a factual brief, and links every statement back to its source page and
line. Missing information and conflicting company names move to a separate
queue. A person reviews the sources before approving; filing is another step.

The security layer is concrete: matter-scoped source reads, selected retained
fields, changed-source checks, and logged human decisions. No autonomous legal
advice, client acceptance, or outbound email.

On ten isolated held-out fictional matters, the deterministic parser classified
18/18 documents, extracted 100/100 fields, and produced 58/58 source-linked
summary statements. Those are fixture results, not claims about real client
work or general legal-document accuracy.

Concept build. Fictional data only. Local prototype; no LLM active.
