# Recording script — The matter room (3–4 minutes)

Status: script prepared; no Loom recording or hosted video published.
Use fictional seed 73 and a fresh runtime database. Show the whole browser at
1512×982, then zoom into the actual source line. Keep the fictional-data label visible.

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem

“Before a lawyer reviews a new matter, someone has to organize the email,
identify the documents, find missing information, and assemble the context.
This Selerim concept handles that preparation, using fictional materials only.”

Show the review desk. Point to the 30 matters, 12 incomplete and 18 complete.
Explain that ‘complete’ means the configured fields are present, not that a
client is accepted or a legal conflict check has passed.

## 0:30–1:20 — The source-backed brief

Open MAT-001, Alder Quay LLC. Read the stated request. Click its source citation.
Show the exact highlighted line in the intake form. Open the engagement-letter
citation to demonstrate that each claim points to its own document. Show the
original PDF. Say: “These are extracted facts, not legal advice. The reviewer
can inspect the source before relying on the brief.”

## 1:20–2:10 — Incomplete matter

Open Needs information, then MAT-008 (company conflict) or MAT-004 (missing
engagement letter). Explain the specific flagged issue. There is no approve
button for an incomplete matter. Click Route to follow-up; show the status.
“No email is sent. This records the route in the local queue for a person to act.”

## 2:10–3:00 — The human decision

Return to MAT-001. Review sources, then check the review attestation. Approve the
summary. Pause on the separate File reviewed intake action. File it and show the
filed view. Explain that local filing does not accept a client or create a legal
engagement. Open Activity log to show the source-view, approval and filing events.

## 3:00–3:40 — Security and measured result

“Sources stay within their matter. The runtime retains selected fields and
citations, not duplicate email and PDF bodies. Changed evidence blocks filing.
Every action is traceable in the local log.”

“On ten isolated held-out fictional matters: 18 of 18 document classifications,
100 of 100 fields, and 58 of 58 source-linked summary statements. This measures
our deterministic fixture pipeline; it is not an accuracy claim for real legal
work or an LLM. This build has no live model, external email or real client data.”

End on the source-backed brief, with the Selerim concept label visible.
